The Firm

Serious counsel,
close to home.

George Makhafola Attorneys Inc is a Mokopane law firm serving clients across Limpopo and beyond. We act for families administering the estates of loved ones, for communities asserting their right to land, and for individuals and businesses navigating disputes that matter to their lives.

Our promise is in our name on the wall: we definitely do our best — prepared thoroughly, communicated plainly, and pursued to the end.

Practice Areas

What we act in.

Estates

Administration of Deceased Estates

Steering estates through the Master's Office from report to final distribution, so that a loved one's wishes — and the law — are honoured without needless delay.

Family

Divorce & Family Law

Divorce, custody, maintenance and family disputes handled with firmness in court and compassion in the consultation room.

Civil

General Civil Litigation

Contractual claims, property disputes and civil actions — argued in the Magistrates' and High Courts on careful pleadings and thorough preparation.

Land

Land Claim Matters

Guiding claimants and communities through restitution processes, protecting rights at every procedural step.

Pension

Pension Law

Advice and representation on pension fund disputes and compliance, for employees and employers alike.

Communal

Communal Property Associations

Formation, governance and dispute resolution for CPAs — keeping communally held land managed lawfully and fairly.

Municipal

Local Government & Municipal Matters

Representing clients in dealings with municipalities and local authorities: compliance, permits, service disputes and review proceedings.
